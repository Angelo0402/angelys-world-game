# Power-Up Star Sprites - ChatGPT Prompt

Generate a sprite sheet for a **blue shining power-up star** for a 2D mobile platformer game (Angely's World).

## Requirements

- **Transparent background** (NOT black, NOT white - fully transparent PNG)
- **20 frames** of animation in a single sprite sheet
- **Dynamic and eye-catching**: the star should sparkle, pulse, rotate slightly, and emit glowing particles
- **Blue color scheme**: bright cyan/blue with white highlights, glowing aura
- **Details**: add small sparkles, light rays, and a soft outer glow around the star
- Each frame should be **220x220 pixels**
- Arrange frames in a **5 columns x 4 rows** grid (1100x880 total)
- The star should be centered in each frame with room for the glow effect
- Style: cartoonish but polished, suitable for a kids' game (the game is for a 7-year-old girl)

## Animation

The 20 frames should show a smooth looping animation:
- Gentle pulsing (scale 95% to 105%)
- Sparkling particles appearing and fading around the star
- Subtle rotation shimmer
- Glow intensity varying

## Do NOT

- Do NOT use a black background
- Do NOT use a white background  
- Do NOT make it look flat or simple - it needs depth, glow, and sparkle details
- Do NOT include text or numbers in the image
