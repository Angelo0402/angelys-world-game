# Star Gem Sprites - ChatGPT Prompt

Generate a sprite sheet for **collectible star gems** for a 2D mobile platformer game (Angely's World).

## Requirements

- **Transparent background** (NOT black, NOT white - fully transparent PNG)
- **20 frames** of animation in a single sprite sheet
- **Gem/crystal style**: faceted star-shaped gems that look precious and collectible
- **Color variants**: create a rich blue/purple gem with inner light refraction
- **Details**: 
  - Faceted surfaces with light reflections
  - Inner glow/core that pulses
  - Small sparkle effects
  - Subtle floating/bobbing motion
- Each frame should be **220x220 pixels**
- Arrange frames in a **5 columns x 4 rows** grid (1100x880 total)
- The gem should be centered in each frame
- Style: polished and precious, like a treasure - suitable for a kids' game

## Animation

The 20 frames should show a smooth looping animation:
- Gentle floating up and down
- Light gleam sweeping across facets
- Inner glow pulsing
- Occasional sparkle bursts

## Do NOT

- Do NOT use a black background
- Do NOT use a white background
- Do NOT make it look flat - needs depth, facets, and light play
- Do NOT include text or numbers in the image
